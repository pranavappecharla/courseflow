Rules Worth Not Forgetting
Hard Rules
  • 120 units to graduate; 45 must be in residence
  • 19 credits is the fall/spring maximum without an overload petition
  • 2.0 cumulative GPA, and 2.0 across all courses applied to the major
  • No Pass/Fail anywhere in the major or in Gen Ed
  • A grade below C does not satisfy a prerequisite
  • 60 units must sit within CICS or the College of Natural Sciences
  • IE and JYW must both be taken at UMass Amherst
  • IE course must fall in your junior or senior year
  • Gen Ed allows at most 3 I/SI courses and 1 CS course
  • 45 graded residence units for Latin Honors
Prerequisite Chains That Bite
  • CICS 110 → 160 → 210 → almost everything else
  • MATH 131 → 132 gates 233, 235, COMPSCI 240 and 250
  • COMPSCI 198C (1 cr) gates COMPSCI 230
  • CICS 210 gates CICS 256 — and sophomores register before juniors
  • COMPSCI 250 gates COMPSCI 311
  • COMPSCI 311 at B+ gates COMPSCI 514
  • COMPSCI 240 at B+ waives STATISTC 315 for COMPSCI 589
  • COMPSCI 220 gates 320, 326 and 485
  • CICS 305 needs ENGLWRIT 112 plus 220, 230 and 240

How It Works
  • Never approved in your first semester; you are eligible from Spring 2027 once fall grades post
  • Any Incomplete (INC) grade blocks approval
  • Submit the CICS Credit Overload Petition before add/drop ends
  • Limits are raised only after everyone has registered, and may not show in SPIRE until the first day of classes. Register for 19 first, then add the extra class after approval if it still has seats
Example: your locked 19 in Spring 2027 plus MATH 235 (3) would be 22 credits and need a 3.5 fall GPA.
