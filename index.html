Rules Worth Not Forgetting
Hard Rules
  • 120 units to graduate; 45 must be in residence
  • 2.0 cumulative GPA, and 2.0 across all courses applied to the major
  • No Pass/Fail anywhere in the major or in Gen Ed
  • A grade below C does not satisfy a prerequisite
  • 60 units must sit within CICS or the College of Natural Sciences
  • IE and JYW must both be taken at UMass Amherst
  • IE course must fall in your junior or senior year
  • Gen Ed allows at most 3 I/SI courses and 1 CS course
  • 45 graded residence units for Latin Honors

The Statistics Prerequisite — worth solving early
OIM 301 will not let you enrol without a qualifying statistics course, and that course sits outside the 15 credits. You already have two candidates inside the CS degree:
  • STATISTC 315 — the ARR accepts this in place of MATH 233 for your fourth math course. If it also qualifies for OIM 301, one course covers both. Check the credit count first; swapping MATH 233 for it may cost you a credit against the 120 total.
  • COMPSCI 240 — Reasoning Under Uncertainty — a probability and statistics course in all but name. It may or may not appear on Isenberg's qualifying list.
Neither is confirmed. Get the qualifying list from Isenberg before you finalise the Spring 2028 math choice — that is the decision point, not senior year.
